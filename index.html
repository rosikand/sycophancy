Counterfactual Consistency

A paired-rollout RL objective for moral sycophancy in language models.

Sycophancy in large language models has been measured many ways , mitigated via prompting, inference-time interventions, and DPO , and shown to have measurable downstream effects on user behavior . Yet on one specific dimension, every published mitigation has failed: when an LLM is shown the same moral conflict described from each side, it tends to affirm both parties as not at fault, in roughly half of cases on average across frontier models. This note proposes paired-rollout GRPO with a joint-affirmation penalty: the minimal modification to standard RL post-training that makes cross-prompt consistency the optimization target, rather than a side-effect of lowering per-prompt sycophancy marginals.

Moral sycophancy is a property of the joint distribution over paired outputs. Single-prompt objectives optimize the marginals. Matching the shape of the loss to the shape of the failure is the contribution.

Where we are

Sycophancy was originally framed as agreement with a user's explicitly stated belief: a model told the user is a Republican starts producing more conservative answers . Recent work broadens the phenomenon. ELEPHANT introduces "social sycophancy" along five dimensions (validation, indirectness, framing, moral, and emotional), and shows that frontier models exhibit it pervasively in advice-seeking interactions. A separate study using a live chat platform shows that sycophantic responses measurably reduce users' intent to repair interpersonal conflicts and increase their certainty in their own rightness .

Social sycophancy. Beyond explicit agreement, this includes affirming the user's framing, softening criticism into indirectness, accepting flawed presuppositions, and siding with whoever is speaking in moral conflicts.

The empirical anomaly. Across the sycophancy mitigation literature, one dimension has resisted every method that worked on the others. Moral sycophancy on perspective-flipped pairs barely moves under instruction prepending, perspective shift, truthful ITI, or DPO . This is the residual the method here targets.

A specific failure mode

Cheng et al.'s AITA-NTA-FLIP construction takes Reddit posts from r/AmITheAsshole where human consensus is clearly "you are not the asshole" (NTA), then rewrites the same conflict from the perspective of the other party. A consistent moral judge would say NTA on one side and YTA (you are the asshole) on the other, since the same incident cannot have both parties blameless. A sycophantic model says NTA to whichever speaker is talking.

Prompt A, original (human consensus: NTA) AITA for refusing to help my mum after she said I "know nothing"?
GPT-4o: NTA
Prompt B, flipped (same conflict, other side) AITA for telling my daughter she "knows nothing, she is young and stupid"?
GPT-4o: NTA

The model affirms both the wrongdoer and the wronged. Across 11 frontier models, this joint-NTA event happens in roughly 48% of paired conflicts on average . It is not a minor edge case; it is the modal behavior on this benchmark.

The mitigation gap

ELEPHANT tested four standard sycophancy mitigations against all five dimensions of social sycophancy. The pattern is the same across all four: validation and indirectness drop substantially, framing moves modestly, moral barely moves at all.

The natural reading of this table is "DPO is too weak; let's try something stronger." The argument here is different. The mismatch is not in the strength of the optimizer but in the shape of its loss.

Why DPO can't fix it

DPO conditions on a single prompt:

$$ \mathcal{L}_{\text{DPO}}(x, y_w, y_l) \;=\; -\log \sigma\!\left( \beta \log \frac{\pi(y_w \mid x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y_w \mid x)} - \beta \log \frac{\pi(y_l \mid x)}{\pi_{\text{ref}}(y_l \mid x)} \right). $$

Both log-ratios condition on the same \(x\). The gradient updates the conditional \(\pi_\theta(\cdot \mid x)\). If the dataset contains a counterfactual pair \((x, x')\), DPO processes them as two independent triples; the gradient from one never sees the other. The model can lower \(P(\text{NTA} \mid x)\) and \(P(\text{NTA} \mid x')\) independently, and DPO will count this as success even if the joint event \(\text{NTA on } x \wedge \text{NTA on } x'\) hardly moves.

Why marginals and joints can diverge. If the features that drive NTA on \(x\) (sympathetic narrator, first-person grievance) are the same features in \(x'\) (because \(x'\) is just \(x\) flipped), the two NTA events are positively correlated through shared latent features. Lowering each marginal lowers the joint less than the product of the marginals would suggest.

The structural claim. Moral sycophancy is a property of \(p_\theta(y, y' \mid x, x')\). DPO optimizes \(p_\theta(y \mid x)\) and \(p_\theta(y' \mid x')\) separately. The optimizer is never asked to look at the joint, so the joint is what survives.

The proposed objective

The fix is to make the loss itself a function of the pair. For each \((x, x') \in \mathcal{D}\):

  1. Paired rollouts. Sample \(k\) completions from each side of the pair: \(\{y_i\}_{i=1}^k \sim \pi_\theta(\cdot \mid x)\) and \(\{y'_j\}_{j=1}^k \sim \pi_\theta(\cdot \mid x')\).
  2. Verdict extraction. Force the verdict slot via constrained generation, yielding labels \(v_i, v'_j \in \{\text{YTA}, \text{NTA}\}\).
  3. Joint reward. For each pair of rollouts \((y_i, y'_j)\): $$ r(y_i, y'_j) \;=\; r_q(y_i) + r_q(y'_j) \;-\; \lambda \cdot \mathbb{1}\!\left[v_i = \text{NTA} \,\wedge\, v'_j = \text{NTA}\right], $$ where \(r_q\) is a quality reward (small RM, or zero with KL regularization) and \(\lambda\) controls the joint penalty.
  4. Per-rollout aggregation. Average over partner rollouts, \(R(y_i) = \frac{1}{k}\sum_j r(y_i, y'_j)\), to get a scalar per rollout. The joint information is now embedded in each rollout's reward.
  5. GRPO update . Standard group-relative advantages over the \(2k\) rollouts, PPO-style clipping , KL regularization to the reference policy.

Mechanism: marginals vs. joints

Why should this beat DPO trained on the same paired data? The mechanistic prediction is specific. DPO can lower the marginals \(P(\text{NTA} \mid x)\) and \(P(\text{NTA} \mid x')\) without touching the conditional \(P(\text{NTA on } x' \mid \text{NTA on } x)\). The joint rate falls roughly in proportion to the marginals, which is real progress but bounded by how much per-prompt affirmation the model can surrender before helpfulness collapses.

Paired-rollout GRPO targets the conditional directly. The reward structure makes each gradient step ask: given that this rollout said NTA, what did the partner rollout say? The optimizer cannot satisfy the loss by independently shifting marginals; the only path is to make the joint event itself rare, which means breaking the correlation between counterfactuals.

Method variants and baselines

The contribution rests on a clean four-way comparison. Each baseline is designed to isolate a single property of the proposed objective.

Decision criterion

The kill experiment runs first. If DPO-Consistency on Qwen3-4B reaches roughly the same joint-NTA rate as paired-rollout GRPO, the mechanistic claim is wrong and the project pivots. If it stalls in the same range as Cheng et al.'s DPO-All (well above 0.30), the loss-shape argument has the empirical foothold it needs.

Experimental plan

Base models. Primary experiments on Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 (small enough for fast iteration, recent enough not to be dismissed as outdated). Cross-family transfer on Llama-3.1-8B-Instruct as the secondary, to confirm the method is not Qwen-specific. A within-family scaling check on Qwen3-8B-Instruct if compute permits.

Data. AITA-NTA-FLIP from the ELEPHANT release (1,591 paired prompts), 80/10/10 split at the pair level so paired prompts stay together across train, val, and test.

Why pair-level splits. Splitting at the prompt level would put one half of a pair in train and the other in test, leaking the counterfactual structure across the split.

Headline metric. Joint-NTA rate on the held-out pair-level test split, computed exactly as in Cheng et al. .

Mechanism diagnostics. For every trained method, report marginal NTA on \(x\), marginal NTA on \(x'\), and the conditional \(P(\text{NTA on } x' \mid \text{NTA on } x)\). These three together decompose the headline number into "lowered marginals" and "broken correlation" components.

Helpfulness preservation. AlpacaEval 2 length-controlled win rate (mandatory), Arena-Hard, MT-Bench. The method must not buy joint-NTA reduction by becoming generally less helpful.

Generalization. Cross-dimension on ELEPHANT's other tracks (AITA-YTA validation, OEQ validation/indirectness/framing, SS framing). Cross-domain on non-Reddit moral conflicts derived from ETHICS and false-presupposition questions from Cancer-Myth.

Ablations. Lambda sweep (\(\lambda \in \{0.25, 0.5, 1, 2, 5\}\)) for the helpfulness-vs-consistency trade-off curve. Rollout count \(k \in \{2, 4, 8, 16\}\) for the efficiency frontier. Random-pair ablation: replace counterfactual partners with random in-batch prompts; if the joint penalty alone (not the paired counterfactual structure) were doing the work, this should match the full method. The prediction is that it should not.

Generalization

The method targets the joint-distribution failure mode specifically. We expect partial transfer to other ELEPHANT dimensions (validation, framing) and stronger transfer within the moral-consistency family (non-Reddit conflicts). What we do not expect is for paired training on AITA to fix sycophancy dimensions whose failure mode is structurally different, such as flawed-presupposition handling or pressure-induced capitulation on factual tasks .

Boundary of the contribution. Validation, indirectness, and framing sycophancy each have their own structure and likely require their own scaffolding. The paired-rollout pattern is the right tool when the failure mode admits a counterfactual structure; it is not a general sycophancy fix.

Open questions

  • Beyond binary verdicts. The joint-affirmation indicator requires a YTA/NTA structure. Open-ended sycophancy (general advice, validation language) lacks this. Whether a soft analogue (a paired classifier score on free-form responses) preserves the mechanism is an empirical question.
  • Pair construction at scale. AITA-NTA-FLIP is a hand-curated flip of ~1500 posts. Scaling to ETHICS or open-domain conflicts requires automated pair generation, which itself can introduce sycophancy-shaped artifacts.
  • Cross-cultural generalization. AITA reflects English-language Reddit norms. Whether the trained model maintains consistency under conflicts whose human consensus differs across cultural frames is unknown.
  • Behavioral validation. The paper measures text-level sycophancy. Whether reduced text-level joint affirmation translates to the behavioral effects measured by Cheng et al. (perspective-taking, repair intent) is the natural follow-up and the obvious extended paper.

Summary

Moral sycophancy on perspective-flipped pairs is the residual after every published mitigation. The mechanistic reason is that the failure is a property of the joint distribution over paired outputs, while standard post-training objectives (DPO, single-prompt RL) optimize only the marginals. Paired-rollout GRPO with a joint-affirmation penalty is the minimal modification to the loss shape that makes cross-prompt consistency the optimization target. The contribution is not the optimizer family or a new architecture; it is matching the shape of the loss to the shape of the failure. The same pattern should apply to any sycophancy dimension that admits a counterfactual pair structure.

Related work

Sycophancy diagnostics. Sharma et al. established sycophancy as agreement with a user's stated belief. ELEPHANT generalizes this to social sycophancy across five dimensions, introducing the AITA-NTA-FLIP construction this paper builds on. The prosocial study shows that text-level sycophancy translates to measurable shifts in user behavior. The Verbalized Assumptions paper is a related diagnostic on flawed-presupposition handling.

Sycophancy mitigation. Khan et al. apply DPO to a 1000-prompt sycophancy preference set, the canonical DPO-for-sycophancy paper but operating entirely on Sharma-style explicit-belief sycophancy. Wei et al. show synthetic data alone can reduce sycophancy; ELEPHANT's own appendix tests value-specific DPO on DailyDilemmas, the closest prior comparison on moral sycophancy specifically.

RL for sycophancy. SMART uses RL with adaptive reasoning trajectories for factual sycophancy, where ground-truth answers are available. Pressure-What-Pressure proposes a multi-component GRPO reward for pressure-paired factual sycophancy. The closest in spirit (GRPO with contrastive pairs), but the pair structure is "with vs. without social pressure cues" rather than "perspective flipped," and the reward decomposes into five terms rather than a single joint penalty.

Counterfactual training in NLP. Counterfactual DPO constructs preference pairs from style-flipped prompts but collapses the pairing back into standard per-triple DPO loss. PairCFR uses contrastive learning on paired counterfactual data in classification settings. Neither maintains the pair structure inside the loss function in a generative RL setting.

Where this work sits. The pairing of (a) AITA-NTA-FLIP-style perspective counterfactuals with (b) paired-rollout GRPO and (c) a single joint-affirmation penalty as the training signal is, to the author's knowledge, novel. The mechanistic claim that moral sycophancy is a joint-distribution failure requiring a joint-distribution loss is also novel as an explicit framing, and is what the experimental design above is structured to test.
